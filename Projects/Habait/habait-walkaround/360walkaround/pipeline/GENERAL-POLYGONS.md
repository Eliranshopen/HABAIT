## 3. What the builder derives (deterministic; every number in `scene-full.report.json` → `general`)
- **Facings (68).** The loops stop at the outline stroke's inner edge, the walls at the grey fill: a 2–5 cm band. A facing's front IS the loop edge, buried 2 cm into the wall; an interruption ≤ 25 cm between two backed runs (a gap between raster wall fragments) is bridged. Coverage 86–100 %.
- **Door infills (6).** Fill every wall-free run the door's span touches (o-11: the 75 cm drawn opening around a 61 cm door, and the wall ends the polygons lost), body = the shortest wall chord + the bands, faces 1 mm short of the loops; the cutter (width × 76 cm, sill → head) cuts the plan's door out of it.
- **Pane-joint posts (16)** where a pane end stops 0.2–9.8 cm short of a wall or pane.
- **Door-in-glass frames (1, answer 2c).** A door-kind cutter that takes ≥ 10 cm² of a pane AND leaves glass ≥ 1 cm past one of its sides frames that pane: two 6 cm posts centred on the cut sides and a 6 cm head centred on the door head, in the WALL material, in the pane's own least-squares line, as deep as max(7 cm, the glass's spread), the floor posts sunk 1 cm; built after the booleans (no cutter touches it), not a fill solid, no glazed leaf. 846: o-13 × `facade-11` only (5.73° to the door line, 35.1 → 21.8 cm off it, 7.39 cm deep, clear 125.9 × 212 cm); o-13 × `facade-4` is reported, not framed (wholly inside the span); o-10 never touches `facade-1` (a bbox overlap only — the cut record's `targets` are bbox candidates, not proof of a cut).
- **The fill.** 1 cm raster, closed by 6 cells, holes filled (33,714 cells: kitchen bays, a niche, thresholds); floor fill 0.25 cm under the floors, ceiling fill 0.25 cm over the ceilings; sky = a flood from unroofed outdoor rooms and the building's outline through cells that are not barriers (closed by 6) and not within 12 cm of an indoor loop → on 846 only the railing bands (9,363 cells).
