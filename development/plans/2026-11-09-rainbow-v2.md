**The flow:**
1. landing;
2. `/hold/`: the fly-around, bird's eye, every apartment marked, pick one;
3. 846 «המשיכו אל הדירה» → `/apartment-journey/?from=hold&unit=846&tour=…&return=…`:
   - opens on the hold's own frame and plays the floor reveal by itself: «16» counts up, floor 16 lights, «דירה 846» and the facts;

4. the 360 tour.

«חזרה לבניין» (top bar) and «לכל הדירות בבניין» go back to the hold at the stop (its URL without the orbit film).

**Path decision: `/apartment-journey/`.** `platform/v2/` was relocated there with `git mv`; nothing was deleted. `/v2/` stays as a stub that forwards to the project landing `/` (Eliran 2026-11-09, after opening `/v2/` and seeing the 846 hero: no entry point may be about one apartment; the journey is reached only by picking 846 at the hold, where naming 846 is right). `?intro=hero` remains a test view nobody links to. Reason: a product route in the flow needs a name that says what it is, and the hold links to it.

**Kept paths:**

- The building film's «המשיכו אל הדירה» still goes to the tour directly (unchanged; follow-up: route it to the journey too, on Eliran's word).

**iOS sound:** the tour's walkthrough music plays on `window.parent.__habaitAudio`, which a tap must create in the same document. The hold keeps its unlock on «המשיכו אל הדירה» (unchanged) and on the direct «לסיור 360°». **From Eliran's blueprint (Omnya Lab, "tower that sells": a tower photo → a flight video from the tower into one floor through the glass with fixed start/end frames → room clips chained last-frame-to-first → a floor picker where hovering lights a floor, a glass panel with level/rooms/area and «Enter level 15»).**

The rooms section starts under the floor's last screen and shows itself only once pinned, on the identical frame, so there is no cut.
2. **Room-to-room as one walk — taken.** Each room:
   - lands looking the way the visitor came in;
   - turns in 6° steps of its gated panorama until the next doorway is ahead (the tour's own doorway hotspots; bearing(u) = centre + (u − 0.5)·360, checked against the room positions);
   - the page pushes through the doorway into the next room, which lands looking the same way.

   The order follows the tour's doorways end to end: terrace → living → dining → kitchen → bedroom 2 → entrance → (through the living room) main bedroom → (through the wardrobe) main bath. The walk ends turned to the bath mirror.

   163 frames per orientation (≈3.3 MB each, loaded per room as it comes). The filmstrip is kept. The static page keeps one picture per room.
3. **Floor highlight + glass panel at /hold/ — partly.**
   - **Built:** the floor glow. While 846's card is open, a band one floor high glows across the tower's face. It is projected every frame through the hold's own camera like the pills: anchor ± 1.6 m, −7…+31.5 m along the facade tangent.

The card already shows exactly «16 · 3 · 106.72 · 26.15» with «המשיכו אל הדירה», and a second panel would collide with the phone bottom sheet. Renaming the CTA is Eliran's call (the iOS audio comments and checks name «המשיכו אל הדירה»). ## 9. Round 4 (2026-11-10) — one automatic flight from «כניסה לדירה» into the 360 tour
**Ask (Eliran, after reviewing rainbow-v2-test.vercel.app; he loves everything up to choosing the apartment):** «משלב בחירת הדירה אני כן רוצה שיהיה את האנימציה של הקומות עד שמגיעים לקומה של הדירה שנבחרה … אבל אני כן רוצה שיהיה את המשך של הריחוף עד כניסה לסלון הדירה ולא המשך כמו עכשיו שאני צריך לגלול למטה. כלומר משלב לחיצה על "המשיכו אל הדירה" יש את הריחוף עם האנימציה של הקומות ואז זה ממשיך לסיור 360 של הדמיית הדירה». Worktree `.wt/journey-r4` (off `82c2422`).

**The sequence (about 9 s, no scrolling):** it starts in /hold/ on 846's «כניסה לדירה» (phones) / «המשיכו אל הדירה» (desktop).
1. **Floors (0.4–3.4 s):** the counter runs up to «16» beside the tower. Floor 16 glows (the band and pin «846»), then «דירה 846» and the facts appear (3 rooms · 106.72 m² · terrace 26.15 m²), as in Eliran's screenshot.
2. **Glide (4.6–7.6 s):** the floor is isolated, then one continuous push toward 846's terrace.
3. **Landing (7.2–8.0 s):** the living room, as the tour opens it.
4. **Hand-off (≈ 8.2 s):** the hold cross-fades to the 360 tour.

«דלג לסיור» skips at any moment. Reduced motion: no flight, the tour at once.

**How:**
- **The journey runs inside the hold.** It plays in a full-screen frame over the hold, starting on the hold's exact on-screen frame box (`?fr=`, the phone framing included), so the tap does not cut. It tells the hold to load the tour behind it at 3.4 s and to show it at the landing. The tour frame is the hold's own (like the direct descent path), so the walkthrough music keeps the AudioContext the visitor's tap made in the hold. Opened on its own, the page goes to the tour itself.
- **The landing is the tour's first view, drawn in the page.**
  - It is a rectilinear reprojection on a canvas from the very panorama the tour loads (`floor=parquet` → `panos-parquet/04-living.webp`).
  - It uses the tour's own first-view maths: room living, yaw −40 (the hand-off URL's `yaw`, set by the hold's `JOURNEY_YAW`), pitch −2, hfov = the tour's `fitHfov()` for that screen (60° on phones, 111.4° at 1440).
  - So the cross-fade shows the same picture on both sides. The tour's walkthrough overlay then opens over it, as on every tour open.
- **Back from the tour:** the tour's «חזרה לבניין» goes to the hold at the stop (its `?return=`, without the film).
- **Archived, not deleted:** the round-3 scroll page (walk, filmstrip, cutaway, 3D dialog) and its media moved to `Habait/platform/tools/_unused/apartment-journey-r3/` (never deployed). The media script now makes only the hold-frame stills.
