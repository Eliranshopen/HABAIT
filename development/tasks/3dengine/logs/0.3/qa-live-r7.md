## Defects
**D5 (Low) A runner started while another runner is finishing the job takes `runner.pid` of a job that is already `done`. Breaks section 12 item 6 ("A runner started for a job that has ended leaves at once and does not take runner.pid. (Started while another runner was finishing the job, it used to hold the pid file of a done job for a moment.)").**
Steps (`s6-pidrace.mjs`, `node s6-pidrace.mjs <rounds> <extras> <seed> early`): temp home, `stub.stepSeconds` 0.1, a job started and approved; when it is in `gating` (two stages from the end) 5 or 6 more runners are spawned with `spawnRunnerDefault`; after `job.json` says `done` the pid file is polled every millisecond for 1.5 s. Expected: it never names one of the extra runners. Actual: it does, for 0 to 16 ms, in 8 of 145 rounds (3/60 with the spawns spread over the last stages, 3/40 and 2/45 with the spawns all in `gating`). Cause: `runRunner` reads the job (not terminal), then `claimPid` takes the job lock and, with the first runner's pid file already given back, writes its own pid; `claimPid` does not look at the state again under the lock. The runner then returns `done` and gives the file back, so the effect is a few milliseconds of `runner.alive: true` on a done job and a pid file that names a runner of no use. Severity Low (no write to the job, no money), but the contract states this case in so many words.

**D6 (Low) A `ledger.json` that parses but names another job in `job_id` (or has none) is not "another shape": the runner writes the job's paid rows into that other file. Breaks section 15 item 3 ("has another shape, cannot be read ... a runner that starts on such a job stops at once ... runs no stage and pays nothing") and, for the other job, section 15 item 3's "`ledger.json` is the only record of what was paid".**
Steps (`s5-wrongid.mjs`): job X done with 3 settled rows; job S at `skinning` with a marked reserved row, runner killed; S's `ledger.json` replaced by the same document with `job_id` = X's id and `rows: []` (variant 2: `{"rows":[]}`, no `job_id`); a real runner started on S. Expected: it stops at once, nothing written. Actual: 3 of 3 (each variant): the runner runs `skinning` (attempt 2 started), its reserve is written by `writeLedger` to the path built from the document's own `job_id`: variant 1 overwrote X's ledger (3 settled rows, $1.8, became 1 reserved row), variant 2 created `jobs/undefined/ledger.json`; then `confirm` reads S's own file, finds no row (`Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'status')`), the job goes `failed` with `STAGE_FAILED`. Nothing was sent (0 sends). Also seen in s3: `{"rows":[1,2]}`, `{"rows":[{}]}` and `{"rows":[]}` are read as an empty ledger (total 0, the copy of $1.8 on a done job is overwritten by 0) and the same failed run follows. Cause: `readLedger` accepts any JSON with an iterable `rows`; `writeLedger` trusts `doc.job_id`. Needs a hand-edited file, hence Low; the fix is to check shape and `job_id` when reading.
