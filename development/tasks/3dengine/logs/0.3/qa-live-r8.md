## Observations
- **O-1 Gate flake, 1 of 10 `npm test`.** m0.2 `after` hook: `rmSync` of the temp home gives EPERM (5 short retries), and a static server of that home stayed alive afterwards. Section 14 item 6 / 11 item 1 want a gate that passes every time; this did not repeat in 9 runs. The `after` does not wait for the killed runners to exit before removing the folder, and kills only the server named in `serve.json`.
- **O-2 "did not leave by themselves" x5 in 225 storm scenarios.** In every case the job was `done`, `runner.pid` absent, and the pid belonged to a runner I had hard-killed or lost track of: the runner log ends abruptly mid-stage (no `stopped:` line) and, when I looked (twice), the pid answered `process.kill(pid, 0)` while `Get-Process` and the command-line query could not read it (a process of my own user is always readable), so I believe the pid was recycled after my kill. Not proven; I did not reproduce it with my kill list excluded (45 more scenarios: 0). Treated as a mistake of my script, not an engine behaviour.
- **O-3 Answers kept lossy.** `NaN`, `Infinity`, `undefined` become `null`; a `Date` becomes a string; a `Map` becomes `{}`; a function member is dropped. The first call gives the stage its original, a cache hit later gives the lossy copy. Section 16 item 4 lists BigInt, circular, function; the contract is silent on these.
- **O-4 `manifest.json` / `job.json` naming another job or none (same family as D6, outside section 16 item 2, which speaks of the ledger).** A runner started on a job whose `manifest.json` has no `job_id` created `jobs/undefined/` and wrote its row there (reproduced in 2 of 2 runs, `appendRow` -> `writeManifest` uses the id inside the document); a `manifest.json` naming a done job changed that job's manifest; a `job.json` naming another job made a runner rewrite that other job's `job.json`, one without `job_id` created `jobs/undefined/`. No contract line covers it; the brief's wording of claim C ("create a folder that is not a job's") is met for the ledger only.
- **O-5 A call whose ledger went bad after its mark is still sent.** With the file replaced during `PAUSE_AFTER_RESERVE`, the send happens (sends 0 -> 1) and the stage fails afterwards when it cannot record; nothing is written. No read happens between the mark and the send.
- **O-6 A result object whose `answer` getter throws** leaves the row `reserved` with its mark (the answer in hand is lost, next attempt orphans it and sends again). Same class as 13.3's "only after that is the answer lost"; unreachable from the stub.
- **O-7 Test speed under a hammering reader.** A process that reads `job.json` in a tight loop makes the runner's atomic rename fail with EPERM for tens of seconds (retries of 3 s x 20). Windows behaviour; a bridge polling at a normal rate is not affected.
