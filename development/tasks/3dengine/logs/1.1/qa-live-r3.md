## Defects
### D-1 (low). A locked upload is a JSON-RPC error, not `NOT_A_PDF`. Section 1 ("Failures": "The upload cannot be read at the moment of the look ... -> NOT_A_PDF, before the scanner starts")
- Why: `checkPdfPath` (engine/inbox.mjs, `fs.openSync`) and the handlers' own `fs.readFileSync(f.path)` in `mcp/stub-engine.mjs` run before `inspectPdf`, outside the `NOT_A_PDF` mapping that `inspectPdf` has for its own read. A file that exists but that another process holds with no sharing (what a writer or a scanner of the upload may do on Windows) therefore leaves as a raw file error.
- Reproduce: `node C:\Users\elira\AppData\Local\Temp\qa-m11-r3-s\b7.mjs` (section 1; copy `qa3-script-b7.txt`): a temp engine home, the 846 PDF in its inbox, `[IO.File]::Open(path,'Open','ReadWrite','None')` held by a PowerShell process, then `inspect_plan` and `start_simulation` through the real server.
- Output: `locked upload inspect_plan: RPC -32603 internal error in inspect_plan: a file error of the engine (EBUSY)`; same for `start_simulation`. After the lock is released the same call answers normally.
- Counter-reading: the line describes `inspectPdf`'s own read, and `checkPdfPath` is milestone 0.2's, unchanged. The code follows that reading. Against it: the contract's closing sentence of section 5 and the task's own rule that every case a section lists has its reason code; "cannot be read at the moment of the look" is exactly a locked file. No path leaves (section 5's no-path line holds). A fix is small: catch an errno in the two handlers' read and in `checkPdfPath`'s open and answer `NOT_A_PDF`.

### D-2 (low). The CLI's `job` reads and writes through a job whose manifest is not its own. Section 5 ("A job's files are read only when they are this job's"; "nothing is written to such a job"; "the CLI prints the job and the reason and exits 1")
- Why: `mustRead` in `engine/cli.mjs` uses `readJob` (job.json only), not `readOwnJob`. `shown()` then runs `withTrueCost`, which calls `syncSpent` and rewrites `job.json` when the ledger's settled total differs from the copy; only afterwards does `readBooks` check the manifest and exit 1. With `--json` the manifest is never checked: the job is printed and the exit is 0. `resume`, `ledger`, `jobs`, every tool and every runner are clean (49 CLI commands, 512 tool calls).
- Reproduce: `node C:\Users\elira\AppData\Local\Temp\qa-m11-r3-s\b4.mjs` (copy `qa3-script-b4.txt`). By hand: a temp home with a job in `awaiting_approval`; `manifest.json` = `{"schema":"habait/manifest.v1","job_id":"ffffffffffffffff","rows":[]}`; `ledger.json` with one settled row of $1 for the job (job.json says `spent_usd` 0); `node engine/cli.mjs job <id>` with `ENGINE_HOME` and `ENGINE_JOBS_DIR` set.
- Output: `job 04762ca47a173685 -> exit 1  WROTE: 04762ca47a173685/job.json | job: ledger.json or manifest.json of job ... cannot be read (job ...: manifest.json is not this job's file (it names job "ffffffffffffffff"))`; then `job <id> --json -> exit 0` printing the job. Same with a manifest that is garbage, a folder, or has a bad row (4 of 4).
- Counter-reading: the 0.3 design says `job <id> --json` "still prints the job file" when the books cannot be read (the message in `readBooks` says so), and section 5's CLI sentence can be read as being about `resume` only (it follows the `startOrResume` clause). I accept that reading for the exit code of `--json`. I do not see text that allows the WRITE: a read command that rewrites `job.json` of a job whose manifest is not its own is the same family as round 2's D-2 and the "nothing is written to such a job" line.
