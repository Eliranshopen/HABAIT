When several decisions are open, Eliran wants them as **questions with selectable options and a free-text option** (the question dialog), each with a recommendation — not as a numbered list in the chat.

His words, 2026-11-17, Kedma D/1: «אולי תשלח לי את כל הסעיפים הפתוחים שלך ואענה בצורה מסודרת» — I sent a numbered list — «אבל שלח לי את זה כשאלות עם אופציית בחירה או מלל חופשי». Sixteen items were then settled in four dialogs within minutes, after an hour of chat had not settled them.

**Why:** he decides fast when each item stands alone with its options and what each one costs or changes; a long list in chat makes him do the sorting. (My reading.)

**How to apply:** collect every open decision, ask them in dialogs of up to four, the items that block work first; each question self-contained (he may see only the dialog), options short, the recommended one first and marked «(מומלץ)», costs in the option text. For a choice of look, show two real pictures of his own room and then ask. This does not replace [[feedback-research-phase-no-asks]]: during research the questions still live in the plan document. See also [[feedback-right-from-the-start-no-piecemeal-fixes]].
