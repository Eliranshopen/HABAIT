When Eliran asks for research and a plan, the deliverable is the plan. Don't end replies with action requests ("contact fal sales", "open API accounts", "say start B1"). Eliran, 2026-11-08: "כרגע אנחנו רק בונים מחקר ותכנית למה אתה מבקש ממני לבצע פעולות?"

**Why:** He separates the planning phase from the execution phase. Asks for actions or go-decisions while planning read as pressure and as noise.

**How to apply:** Put the open decisions in the plan's "questions for Eliran" section and mention them in at most one line. Don't propose next execution steps or ask for a go until he says he is moving to execution. Related: [[feedback-delegated-harness-calls]] (short replies).
