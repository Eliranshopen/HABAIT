In the 360 tour the PHONE has no walking arrows (no pad, no floor arrows, no joystick — all three were built and removed on 2026-11-11); it moves by the door rings, the room strip and the prev/next pill. The DESKTOP keeps the keyboard's arrow keys (up = a step to the way ahead, left / right = turn while held, down = a step back, each press named on the screen) and a click on the picture.

**Why:** by "walk like a person" Eliran means CONTINUOUS movement — a step from where he stands, stopping anywhere. A tour of 11 fixed 360 pictures can only jump between 11 viewpoints, so every arrow design felt wrong to him («עדיין לא מתנהגים כמו הליכה רציפה … אלא מפנים לחלק של כל חדר»). Offered and not taken: a depth-warp moving transition ($0, unproven), dozens of extra viewpoints (hundreds of dollars), a 3D-model walk.

**How to apply:** when he asks for movement "like walking", say first, in one line, that fixed pictures cannot do continuous walking and name the three real routes — before building any control. Four builds were spent on 2026-11-11 because I built controls instead of saying this. Do not re-add walking arrows on the phone unless he asks. Record: C:/RnD/memory/project/2026-11-11-tour-point-move.md. Related: [[rule-mobile-and-desktop]], [[feedback-realism-over-diagram-look]].

2026-11-12 (later): he REJECTED pre-made walking films too («אני לא רוצה סרטון בהדמייה אלא אני רוצה תחושה אמיתית של הליכה בדירה לפי החיצים שאני אזיז»). What he wants = interactive, real-time free walking steered by his own arrows, photoreal, no warping. That needs a true 3D scene (the plan-exact Blender model in the browser, or a photoreal 3D reconstruction such as Gaussian splats) — not pictures, not films. Reprojected pictures (/walk/) warp; films are not steerable.

2026-11-12: the ROOM BUTTONS (not walking controls) are see-through FLOOR ARROWS in one tidy row with small plain names — Eliran rejected dark rings with boxed names as cluttered. Every room seen in a picture gets one, and each must be visible at the level view (no tilting) on desktop and phone.
