**How to apply.**
- "Reengineering a site" means: study it and write down its motion and presentation ideas. It never means reproducing its palette, fonts, grid, section order, copy, buttons or media, or matching its timings 1:1.
- Every side-by-side must show the difference. Test: someone from the reference's company should not recognise their site.
- The reanimate skill clones by design. Use it to measure, never as the target.
