# Apartment selection

**Selected: model Hatzav (דגם חצב).** A single-storey detached house with a garden: 3 rooms, 80 m², Moshav Na'ama, Harei Zahav. Eliran confirmed the selection on 2026-11-17 ("כן רוץ").

| Candidate | Rooms | Area | Source | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hatzav (חצב)** | **3** | **80 m²** | brochure p.5 and the colour plan PNG (identical) | **the only 3-room model: selected** |

| Rotem (רותם) | 7 | 200 m² | brochure pp.8–9 | no |

## Why the selected variant is reliable
- **Two independent copies agree.** The PNG and brochure p.5 were overlaid after rotating p.5 by 90°. Walls, openings, printed numbers and furniture match.
- **Two differences between them:**
  - p.5 has a pale green rectangle over the dining table, a print artefact.
  - p.5 is lower resolution.
- **The PNG is the master** because it is sharper and drawn at exactly 1 px = 1 cm.
- **The room count follows the Israeli convention:** living space + bedroom + safe room = 3 rooms. The sheet prints 39 + 12 + 9 m² for these.
- **No layouts were mixed.** Ela and Eshel share Hatzav's north block (safe room, bathroom, entrance; 540/510/1050 dimensions), but nothing was taken from them.

## Not decidable from the sources
- **The lot is not known.** Which of the ~60 lots on the site plan (p.3) carries a Hatzav house is not stated.
- **The house's orientation to north is not known.** The view and the sun direction are therefore ASSUMED. The sun is placed to light the deck side.
