The tour is RAINBOW's own viewer (`tour360/viewer/tour.js`, `edit-live.js`, `server/handler.mjs`) built by RAINBOW's
`tools/build-tour.mjs` for the apartment `tour360/apartments/hatzav` (generated by its `prep.mjs` from this job, $0).
RAINBOW's interface and features only: the panoramas, the rings, the catalog items and the narration are Hatzav's own; no RAINBOW
furniture, item or catalog picture is in it (`apartments/hatzav/catalog/items.json` = the job's 24 AI-made alternatives).

Evidence: `qa/360/real/` = the FINAL build (the realistic set, `--full`, 10 rooms) and `qa/360/blender/` = the Blender-set build;
each has `shots.json` (every shot's page state), the shots `desktop-1440-*.png` (D-) and `phone-390-*.png` (P-, touch), and
`overlay-<room>.jpg` (ring outlines yellow, door markers blue, drawn on the panorama). Smoke: `tools/smoke.mjs hatzav --catalog --full`
PASS — 10 scenes · 26 doors · 10/10 2:1 · disclosure 3/3 ×3 · catalog 24 · 27 rings in 5 scenes · full set 10 · 15.5 MB.
